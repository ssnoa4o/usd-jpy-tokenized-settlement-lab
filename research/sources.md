Verification caveat: pages were read through a fetch tool that returns a model-made summary, not raw text. Wording must be re-checked in a browser before any verbatim quote. "Verified (HTML summary)" means the page was opened and its claims were noted from that summary. Most PDFs could not be read (binary, no text extraction). Status key: verified = page opened and used for the stated claims; partially verified = only part was read (abstract, index, landing page); UNVERIFIED = not opened or content not readable. UNVERIFIED items must not be cited for facts.
